What each list is

Legislative Watch
City councils and county commissions across Knox and the surrounding counties, the Tennessee General Assembly, state courts and agencies, and the Federal Register. No fixed schedule: we only write when there is a citable deadline or a real development worth watching.
News Watch: what it covers
This is DSA news, not general news: national leadership, endorsements and elections, internal caucus fights, and anything touching Knoxville or Tennessee. Pick one mode below, or skip it.
News Watch: the daily temperature
Every DSA story we find, commentary and reaction included, sent whenever anything turns up.
News Watch: only real developments
No commentary. Nothing until something actually happens: a vote, an endorsement, an election result.
