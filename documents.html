Chapter documents

What the chapter runs on, in full, so any member can read it before a vote instead of after one.

Before the membership now

A complete revision of the chapter’s bylaws is under consideration, together with the motion that would carry it into effect. Under the current bylaws a revision has to pass at two consecutive meetings. There is time to read it, and time to argue with it.

If you would rather start short, the overview and the summary of changes cover the same ground in a fraction of the words. Where an overview and the bylaws differ, the bylaws govern.

Harassment and grievance

If something has happened, you do not need any of this to start. Write to us through contact and it goes straight to the elected Harassment Grievance Officer, confidentially. These are here so you can read the process before you are in it, and file on paper if you would rather.

  • Harassment and Grievance Officers: procedures

    How the chapter appoints its Harassment Grievance Officers, what they do with a complaint, and what accountability they are held to. Written against DSA National’s old Resolution 33; where it conflicts with the current national policy below, the national policy controls.
