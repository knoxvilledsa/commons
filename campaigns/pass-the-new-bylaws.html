The chapter still runs on bylaws written in 2016. The Rules and Bylaws Working Group spent this year drafting a replacement. It is a lean set of bylaws, plus the codes, rules, and policies that make them work. All 27 documents are written. Now they come to you. Each one goes to a General Meeting for discussion and a vote, and nothing changes until members say so. Come to a working group meeting if you want to read along or help.

Rules and Bylaws Working Group

The Rules and Bylaws Working Group drafts and reviews the chapter’s Bylaws and Rules documents, including the bylaws revision’s sub-documents. It is a time-limited working group, not a standing committee, and it will conclude once the bylaws revision is complete.
