Where the campaign stands

400 members by year’s end

56%

223 of 400 members

Every member makes the chapter stronger: more people to knock doors, pack hearings, staff a table, and win the fights that matter in East Tennessee. We have set a goal of 400 members by year’s end, and we are growing every month. Dues start where you can, and waivers are no questions asked, so money is never the reason someone cannot join. If you believe Knoxville should work for working people, this is your invitation. Join us, and bring a friend.

Membership Retention

The Membership Retention Committee makes sure nobody who joins the chapter gets lost. It welcomes new members, follows up after a first meeting, and helps people find their place. It also runs the Grow the Chapter campaign.

Meets at the Monthly Workshop, second Monday of the month (location to be decided soon)·membership_retention@knoxvilledsa.org
