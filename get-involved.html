Rules and Bylaws Working Group

The Rules and Bylaws Working Group drafts and reviews the chapter’s Bylaws and Rules documents, including the bylaws revision’s sub-documents. It is a time-limited working group, not a standing committee, and it will conclude once the bylaws revision is complete.

Also every month

Meetings anyone can come to

The whole chapter meets at the monthly General Meeting to make decisions together. On the second Monday of every month, there is a hands-on workshop to build organizing skills and plan the work. Both are open to the public, and both are a good first stop.
