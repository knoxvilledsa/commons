About

A chapter run by its members

Knoxville Area DSA is the local chapter of the Democratic Socialists of America. We are your neighbors, and we organize for a Knoxville that works for working people across East Tennessee.

Where we stand

What we believe

We are socialists, and we believe the people who do the work should run the economy and the government. Knoxville does not have to be a place where rent, healthcare, and bus fare are decided by people who never ride the bus. Capitalism made these problems, and organized neighbors can unmake them.

How the chapter runs

Democratic, and open to everyone

The chapter makes its decisions together. Once a month the whole chapter gathers at the General Meeting to set direction, vote, and hear from committees. Meetings are open to the public, and you do not have to be a member to come, speak, or just listen.

Between General Meetings, most of the work happens in committees and at a monthly workshop, where members organize around the issues they care about. New folks are always welcomed and walked through how things work, in the room and in the chat.

What we organize for

Everyday organizing, close to home

Right now the chapter is organizing around public transit, keeping frequent and affordable KAT service on the agenda, and around political education, building a shared understanding of democratic socialism through regular study and discussion. A monthly workshop brings members together to learn organizing skills and plan.

None of this requires experience. It requires showing up, and there is a place for you when you do.
