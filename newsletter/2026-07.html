The Knoxville Free Press: July 2026

Thursday, July 23, 2026

Welcome back to The Knoxville Free Press, the monthly newsletter of the Knoxville Democratic Socialists of America (DSA).

If you’re interested in joining the work of the Knoxville DSA, please join us at our next monthly meeting (the fourth Monday of each month) or consider joining one of our working groups. Please see the end of the newsletter for other ways to get involved.

Tell Us What You Think of This Newsletter

We would like to hear your feedback. Are there sections you skip, or ones you want more of? Take a minute to fill out our feedback form. We’ll use what you tell us to shape next month’s issue. This is an anonymous form.

Chapter Business, Committees and Working Groups

At our June general meeting, 20 members participated. The meeting opened with community agreements and introductions, and a member took the chair.

A member introduced a set of proposed bylaws drafted by our Treasurer. The room had a lot of questions about the length and detail of the new bylaws, so a member proposed forming a Bylaws Committee to take a closer look before anything comes to a vote.

The chapter also voted by consensus to apply for a Chapter Development Grant, a small but real step toward giving Knox DSA more capacity to do the work we want to do. If approved, this money could go toward things like childcare and translation at meetings, tech for hybrid meetings, new member orientations, and space or materials for canvasses and political education events. It’s a chance to make our meetings and campaigns more accessible and effective.

A member reported on the working group collaboration meetings held on second Mondays. Another member raised a good question about how working groups should communicate day to day, whether that is through Google Docs or Signal.

Upcoming Knoxville DSA Events

July General Meeting. Monday, July 27 at 6:00 PM. RSVP at our Action Network.
