Passed around by members

These are things members shared, not chapter positions. Linking an essay is not endorsing it, and a few of them disagree with each other on purpose.

  • The Two Souls of Socialism

    The one that keeps coming back. Draper splits socialism into two traditions, one that arrives from above and one that is built from below, and argues the difference is the whole argument.

    Hal Draper, 1966 (PDF)

    Read
  • Socialists need to plant a flag

    On saying plainly what we are for, rather than only what we are against.

    Left Notes

    Organizing in a bad moment without either despair or pretending.

    A practical list, aimed at people who are angry and want somewhere to put it.

    Robert Reich
